Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All of your data is stored locally on your device. XM Forge has no servers and requires no account. Your financial information never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

Does XM Forge connect to my bank or brokerage?

No. XM Forge is a manual, educational tracker. It does not connect to banks, brokerages, or exchanges, and it never places trades or pulls live prices. You enter and manage your own data.

How do I back up or move my data?

Use the export feature to save your data as a CSV file or a shareable snapshot image. You can store the exported file wherever you like (Files app, iCloud Drive, email, etc.) and re-import or reference it later.

Why does the app ask for camera or photo access?

Camera and photo library access is used only to attach receipt images to your transactions. Images are processed and stored locally on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Does XM Forge work offline?

Yes — completely. Every feature, including all charts and calculators, works without an internet connection.

I deleted the app. Can I recover my data?

Because all data is stored locally and we have no servers, deleting the app also deletes its data. We recommend exporting a CSV backup regularly if your records are important to you.
