Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

XM Forge ("the App") is a private, local-first personal finance toolkit. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. The short version: your data stays on your device, and we collect nothing.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

XM Forge is designed to work entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, store, or have access to any of your personal information, including:

  • Financial data (accounts, holdings, transactions, budgets, goals)
  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers

2. Data Storage

All data you enter into the App — accounts, transactions, receipts, budgets, and goals — is stored locally on your device. No data is synced to any server because the App has no servers and requires no account. If you delete the App, its locally stored data is removed with it.

3. Camera and Photo Library Access

The App may request access to your camera or photo library solely so you can attach receipt images to transactions. Images are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted anywhere.

4. Data Export

You may choose to export your data as a CSV file or a shareable snapshot image. Exports happen only when you explicitly initiate them, and the exported files are handled entirely by you and the share destination you choose. We have no access to exported data.

5. Third-Party Services

XM Forge does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or cloud services. The App makes no network connections for its core functionality.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Because the App collects no data, no update will ever introduce data collection without prominent notice.
